**Hydration**. Process of restoring server-side rendered application on the client.

## Common Hydration Tasks

- Reusing server rendered DOM structures.
- Persisting app state.
- Transferring app data from server to client.
- ...

## Importance of Hydration

- Improves app performance by avoiding extra work to recreate the DOM on the client-side.
- Leads to improved performance as dictated by *Core Web Vitals (CWV)*.
